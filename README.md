# Family ancestry records

Pages summarizing what public records say about our family's Italian citizenship, with images of
the records themselves. `bonina-lisuzzo.html` covers Mom's side (Milazzo and Lascari to Brooklyn and
Albany); `furino-dascole.html` covers Dad's side (Saviano and Nola to Williamsburg, Brooklyn). Built from
free archives (FamilySearch, the NYC Municipal Archives, the Italian State Archives' Antenati portal, the
US National Archives) in October 2026.

- `index.html` — landing page; `bonina-lisuzzo.html` and `furino-dascole.html` — the two family pages (GitHub Pages serves all three)
- `records/web/` — reduced copies of each record for the pages
- `records/full/` — the full-resolution research copies (not certified; for filing, certified
  copies are ordered from the archive named under each record). A few records are scans of papers
  the family kept (marked "kept by the family"), and the Bonina section carries the ancestor
  sheets of a cousin's compiled family tree.

Living relatives, and our grandparents, appear by first name only; census pages that list living relatives
are not published. Research copies, not legal advice. Compiled by Sal with Claude; the private working
files (full tree, search log, checksums) are kept separately.
